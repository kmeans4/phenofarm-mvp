## 4. Stripe assessment

Assessment: PhenoFarm is likely to face a Stripe eligibility issue as US software dedicated to the cannabis industry, even when its only charge is a software subscription.

Stripe's FAQ expressly addresses services that do not sell marijuana but support that industry. When more than 25% of the service targets cannabis sellers/products, it lists Australia, Canada, the EU, New Zealand and the UK as supported regions; the US is absent. This makes the industry focus relevant independently of wholesale payment processing. [Official FAQ](https://support.stripe.com/questions/prohibited-and-restricted-businesses-list-faqs).

The broader list also includes cannabis dispensaries and related businesses. Stripe states that some restricted businesses need detailed review and explicit approval. [Restricted-business list](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses), [review and exceptions](https://support.stripe.com/questions/restricted-business-list-and-considerations).

Request a written decision for the exact model: US B2B software for licensed dispensaries/growers, browsing, negotiations and tracking, with only software subscription fees and no cannabis checkout, money collection or settlement. Disclose the actual workflows and audience. A generic SaaS label or an active Stripe account is not proof that this model is approved. This assessment is an inference from the published rules; Stripe has not reviewed or approved PhenoFarm in this task.

Production still has no Stripe environment variables, and no paid billing was enabled or payment-provider messages sent.
